# FR-140: Select Event Responses While Scheduling A Timed Event

On the [Timed Event Scheduling Page](../../../terminology/glossary.md#timed-event-scheduling-page), an [Event Owner](../../../terminology/glossary.md#event-owner) may select [Event Responses](../../../terminology/glossary.md#event-response) for filtering, and selecting or clearing a response there shall not leave the **Timed Event Scheduling Page**.
While one or more **Event Responses** are selected on the **Timed Event Scheduling Page**, the [Timed Grid](../../../terminology/glossary.md#timed-grid) shall show availability and calculate [Schedule Overlap](../../../terminology/glossary.md#schedule-overlap) only for the selected **Event Responses**.
A selection made on the [Timed Event Page](../../../terminology/glossary.md#timed-event-page) shall remain selected and filtering when the **Event Owner** enters the **Timed Event Scheduling Page**.
Cancelling scheduling shall preserve the selected **Event Responses** and keep the **Timed Grid** filtered to them, while confirming a schedule or clearing the saved [Timed Event Occurrence Span](../../../terminology/glossary.md#timed-event-occurrence-span) shall clear the selection and show every **Event Response**.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Selecting a response on the **Timed Event Scheduling Page** keeps the page in scheduling mode and removes every unselected response from the **Timed Grid** availability and **Schedule Overlap**.
- Clearing the last selected response restores every response's availability and overlap calculation while scheduling remains active.
- A response selected on the **Timed Event Page** is still selected and filtering on the **Timed Event Scheduling Page**.
- Cancelling scheduling leaves the selected responses selected and the **Timed Grid** filtered to them.
- Confirming a schedule through Timeful, Google Calendar, or Outlook clears the selection and shows every response's availability.
- Clearing the saved **Timed Event Occurrence Span** clears the selection and shows every response's availability.
