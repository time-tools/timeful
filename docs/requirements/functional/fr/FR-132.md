# FR-132: Cue The Add Availability Action On Event Response Creation Pages

On a [Timed Event Response Creation Page](../../../terminology/glossary.md#timed-event-response-creation-page) or a [Dates-Only Event Response Creation Page](../../../terminology/glossary.md#dates-only-event-response-creation-page), an [Event Visitor](../../../terminology/glossary.md#event-visitor) who has no editable [Event Response](../../../terminology/glossary.md#event-response) shall see a hint that names the `Add availability` action and states where to find it.

A viewer has no editable [Event Response](../../../terminology/glossary.md#event-response) when they have no response at all or hold only responses they cannot edit, and the hint shall name the `Add availability` action even when the filled `Edit availability` action is disabled.

On a phone viewport, the hint shall read `Add availability (at the bottom of the screen) to show when you're available for this event.`

On a desktop viewport, the hint shall read `Add availability (in the event header) to show when you're available for this event.`

The hint shall not be dismissible, and it shall disappear once the [Event Visitor](../../../terminology/glossary.md#event-visitor) owns an editable [Event Response](../../../terminology/glossary.md#event-response).

The hint shall not appear on an [Archived Event](../../../terminology/glossary.md#archived-event), on an availability group, or on a sign-up form.
