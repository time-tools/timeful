# FR-009: Present Timed Grid States Consistently

[Timed Grids](../../../terminology/glossary.md#timed-grid) shall identify a cell's state with context-specific labels and visually distinct states.

## Acceptance Criteria

- In [Custom Timed Domain Editing](../../../terminology/glossary.md#custom-timed-domain-editing), selected [Active Slots](../../../terminology/glossary.md#active-slots) are white, selectable [Inactive Slots](../../../terminology/glossary.md#inactive-slots) are light grey, and cells outside the [Enabled Domain](../../../terminology/glossary.md#enabled-domain) are dark grey.
- While editing availability in an event availability grid, the [Legend Section](../../../terminology/glossary.md#legend-section) labels `Available (you)`, `If needed (you)`, and `Unavailable (you)`; an [Active Slot](../../../terminology/glossary.md#active-slots) unavailable to the current respondent is pale red.
- Outside availability editing, the [Legend Section](../../../terminology/glossary.md#legend-section) labels the availability states `Available (at least one respondent)`, `If needed (at least one respondent)`, and `Unavailable (all respondents)`.
- The [Legend Section](../../../terminology/glossary.md#legend-section) shows only states that can occur in the current mode and does not expose internal [Enabled Domain](../../../terminology/glossary.md#enabled-domain), [Inactive Slots](../../../terminology/glossary.md#inactive-slots), or domain terminology.
- [Inactive Slots](../../../terminology/glossary.md#inactive-slots) remain editable where the editing mode allows them; dark-grey [Padding Cells](../../../terminology/glossary.md#padding-cell) are non-editable.
- Padding occurs only in cells without a [Slot](../../../terminology/glossary.md#slot) of the [Enabled Domain](../../../terminology/glossary.md#enabled-domain) for that [Projected Date Column](../../../terminology/glossary.md#projected-date-column) and is not created by page-wide filler rows.
