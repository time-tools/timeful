# FR-137: Limit The Grid Pointer To Desktop Hover While Scheduling

On the [Timed Event Scheduling Page](../../../terminology/glossary.md#timed-event-scheduling-page), the [Grid Pointer](../../../terminology/glossary.md#grid-pointer) shall render only while a non-pressing pointer hovers a [Timed Grid](../../../terminology/glossary.md#timed-grid) cell on a desktop viewport.

Clicking, tapping, dragging, or releasing on the **Timed Grid** shall not render or retain the **Grid Pointer**.
The next unpressed pointer movement over a **Timed Grid** cell shall render it again at that cell, including within the cell where the release happened.

On a phone viewport, the **Grid Pointer** shall not render on the **Timed Grid** during scheduling.

## Acceptance Criteria

- On a desktop viewport, moving a non-pressing pointer across the **Timed Grid** during scheduling shows the **Grid Pointer** at the hovered cell.
- On a desktop viewport, clicking, dragging, or releasing on the **Timed Grid** during scheduling hides the **Grid Pointer**; it stays hidden after the release until the next unpressed pointer movement, which shows it at the cell under the pointer, including within the released cell.
- On a phone viewport, tapping, dragging, or releasing on the **Timed Grid** during scheduling never renders the **Grid Pointer**, including a tap on one cell followed by a drag from another cell.
- The pending [Timed Event Occurrence Span](../../../terminology/glossary.md#timed-event-occurrence-span) remains rendered during and after the interaction.
- [Availability Editing](../../../terminology/glossary.md#availability-editing) keeps its existing **Grid Pointer** behavior.
