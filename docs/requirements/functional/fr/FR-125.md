# FR-125: Outline Legend Section Square Items

Every square indicator in the event-page [Legend Section](../../../terminology/glossary.md#legend-section), including the Scheduled event indicator, shall render its outline in the same grey color.

The outline shall be discernible against the indicator's fill.
