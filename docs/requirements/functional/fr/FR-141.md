# FR-141: Gate the Cookie Consent Banner With a Build-Time Flag

The frontend shall mount the cookie-consent banner in the app shell only when `VITE_ENABLE_COOKIE_CONSENT` is explicitly set to a normalized `true` value.

When the flag is unset, blank, `false`, or another value, the frontend shall not mount the banner in the app shell.

When the banner is mounted, its existing consent interactions and browser persistence shall remain available.

The `/cookie-settings` route shall remain available independently of the flag.
