# FR-013: Keep The Display Timezone From Changing Event Data

Changing the [Display Timezone](../../../terminology/glossary.md#display-timezone) shall preserve every [Time Slot's](../../../terminology/glossary.md#time-slot) [Instant](../../../terminology/glossary.md#instant) and shall not change the [Event Timezone](../../../terminology/glossary.md#event-timezone), the [Event Picked Dates](../../../terminology/glossary.md#event-picked-dates), the [Enabled Domain](../../../terminology/glossary.md#enabled-domain), or [Active Slots](../../../terminology/glossary.md#active-slots).

## Acceptance Criteria

- A [Time Slot's](../../../terminology/glossary.md#time-slot) projected date, rendered clock time, tooltip date and time, and [Projected Date Column](../../../terminology/glossary.md#projected-date-column) update for the selected [Display Timezone](../../../terminology/glossary.md#display-timezone).
- A [Time Slot](../../../terminology/glossary.md#time-slot) renders no more than once, including when its displayed time crosses midnight.
- Wrapped ranges remain continuous without structural split gaps.
- Changing the [Display Timezone](../../../terminology/glossary.md#display-timezone) leaves the [Event Picked Dates](../../../terminology/glossary.md#event-picked-dates), the [Enabled Domain](../../../terminology/glossary.md#enabled-domain), and [Active Slots](../../../terminology/glossary.md#active-slots) unchanged.
