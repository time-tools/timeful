# FR-129: Restore An Active Access Transfer After An Event-Page Reload

After the source browser that created an [Access Transfer](../../../terminology/glossary.md#access-transfer) reloads the event page, opening the Manage access dialog shall restore the most recently created saved transfer that is still pending or approved as the current transfer.

A restored pending transfer shall show its transfer link, status, matching-code input, approval control, and cancel control.
A restored approved transfer shall show its status and cancel control without the matching-code input.

A saved transfer that has ended or is unavailable shall not be restored as the current transfer.
The dialog shall present the create-link form instead and shall keep the [Granted Event Visitor Control Credential (Granted EVCC)](../../../terminology/glossary.md#granted-event-visitor-control-credential-granted-evcc) history available for revocation.
When several saved transfers are still active, the most recently created one shall be restored.
