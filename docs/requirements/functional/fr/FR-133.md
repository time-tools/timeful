# FR-133: Place Grid Interaction Instructions At The Top Of The Event Page

During [Availability Editing](../../../terminology/glossary.md#availability-editing) on a [Timed Event Page](../../../terminology/glossary.md#timed-event-page) or a [Dates-Only Event Page](../../../terminology/glossary.md#dates-only-event-page), or during scheduling on a [Timed Event Scheduling Page](../../../terminology/glossary.md#timed-event-scheduling-page) or a [Dates-Only Event Scheduling Page](../../../terminology/glossary.md#dates-only-event-scheduling-page), the interaction instruction for the applicable [Timed Grid](../../../terminology/glossary.md#timed-grid) or [Dates-Only Grid](../../../terminology/glossary.md#dates-only-grid) shall appear at the top of the page content, above the event header and the grid, on every viewport.

A phone viewport shall use `Tap` as the instruction verb, and a desktop viewport shall use `Click`.

The instruction shall not be dismissible, and it shall render only while the applicable editing or scheduling state is active.

Browser-stored dismissal state from an earlier version of the application shall not suppress the instruction.

On a phone viewport, entering [Availability Editing](../../../terminology/glossary.md#availability-editing) shall bring the instruction into view.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The timed available instruction shall read `Tap and drag on the grid below to add your "available" times in green.` on a phone viewport and `Click and drag on the grid below to add your "available" times in green.` on a desktop viewport.
- The dates-only available instruction shall read `Tap and drag on the grid below to add your "available" days in green.` on a phone viewport and `Click and drag on the grid below to add your "available" days in green.` on a desktop viewport.
- The timed if-needed instruction shall read `Tap and drag on the grid below to add your "if needed" times in yellow.` on a phone viewport and `Click and drag on the grid below to add your "if needed" times in yellow.` on a desktop viewport.
- The dates-only if-needed instruction shall read `Tap and drag on the grid below to add your "if needed" days in yellow.` on a phone viewport and `Click and drag on the grid below to add your "if needed" days in yellow.` on a desktop viewport.
- In an availability group, the instruction shall read `Toggle which calendars are used. Tap and drag on the grid below to edit your availability.` on a phone viewport and `Toggle which calendars are used. Click and drag on the grid below to edit your availability.` on a desktop viewport.
- While scheduling a timed event, the instruction shall read `Tap and drag on the grid below to schedule the event during those times.` on a phone viewport and `Click and drag on the grid below to schedule the event during those times.` on a desktop viewport.
- While scheduling a dates-only event, the instruction shall read `Tap and drag on the grid below to schedule the event during those days.` on a phone viewport and `Click and drag on the grid below to schedule the event during those days.` on a desktop viewport.
- The instruction shall have no dismiss control.
- The instruction shall not render while neither [Availability Editing](../../../terminology/glossary.md#availability-editing) nor scheduling is active.
- The instruction shall render when a legacy `closedHintTextedit_availability` key is present in browser storage.
- On a phone viewport, starting [Availability Editing](../../../terminology/glossary.md#availability-editing) shall bring the instruction into view.
