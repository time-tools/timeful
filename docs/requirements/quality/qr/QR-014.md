# QR-014: Require Authenticated PostgreSQL Access In Staging And Production

## Source

An operator connects to the PostgreSQL database of a staging or production deployment.

## Stimulus

A connection or operation attempts to use missing credentials or a role without the privileges its purpose requires.

## Environment

The deployment targets staging or production.
Development and test environments are excluded where their documented isolated configuration intentionally differs.

## Artifact

PostgreSQL access control for the deployment.

## Response

The deployment shall require an authenticated connection for every PostgreSQL access and shall grant each deployment role only the privileges its purpose requires.

## Response Measure

Configuration inspection shows distinct administrative, migration, application, and backup roles for the deployment, and connection tests confirm that a connection without valid credentials is rejected and that each role cannot perform operations outside its purpose.
