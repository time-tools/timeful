# QR-018: Delete Observability Data After Its Retention Window

## Source

The configured [Retention Window](../../../terminology/glossary.md#retention-window) elapses.

## Stimulus

Stored [Observability Data](../../../terminology/glossary.md#observability-data) becomes older than the configured [Retention Window](../../../terminology/glossary.md#retention-window).

## Environment

Development, staging, or production; single-node local-disk storage; the [Retention Window](../../../terminology/glossary.md#retention-window) is configured to a finite 14-day period and not left unlimited.

## Artifact

Stored [Observability Data](../../../terminology/glossary.md#observability-data) and its [Stream](../../../terminology/glossary.md#stream) metadata.

## Response

The system shall delete [Observability Data](../../../terminology/glossary.md#observability-data) that is older than the configured [Retention Window](../../../terminology/glossary.md#retention-window) without operator action.

## Response Measure

Inspection of the stored [Streams](../../../terminology/glossary.md#stream) after the [Retention Window](../../../terminology/glossary.md#retention-window) shows no records older than the window, and a shortened **Retention Window** on a test **Stream** demonstrates the deletion of expired [Observability Data](../../../terminology/glossary.md#observability-data).
