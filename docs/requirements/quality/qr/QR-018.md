# QR-018: Delete Observability Data After Its Retention Window

## Source

The configured retention window elapses.

## Stimulus

Stored observability data becomes older than the configured retention period.

## Environment

Development, staging, or production; single-node local-disk storage; retention is configured to a finite window of 14 days and not left unlimited.

## Artifact

Stored log, metric, and trace data and its stream metadata.

## Response

The system shall delete observability data that is older than the configured retention window without operator action.

## Response Measure

Inspection of the stored streams after the retention window shows no records older than the window, and a shortened retention window on a test stream demonstrates the deletion of expired data.
