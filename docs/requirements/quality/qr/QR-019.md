# QR-019: Isolate Observability Data Across Environments

## Source

A client presents an [Environment-Scoped Observability Credential](../../../terminology/glossary.md#environment-scoped-observability-credential).

## Stimulus

The client attempts to read or ingest [Observability Data](../../../terminology/glossary.md#observability-data) in another environment's organization or [Streams](../../../terminology/glossary.md#stream).

## Environment

Staging and production use one shared OpenObserve instance, and each environment has its own organization, [Streams](../../../terminology/glossary.md#stream), and [Environment-Scoped Observability Credentials](../../../terminology/glossary.md#environment-scoped-observability-credential).
Development runs its own instance outside the shared boundary, and test environments are excluded where their documented isolated configuration intentionally differs.

## Artifact

OpenObserve organization and [Stream](../../../terminology/glossary.md#stream) access control.

## Response

OpenObserve shall authorize each [Environment-Scoped Observability Credential](../../../terminology/glossary.md#environment-scoped-observability-credential) only for its own environment's organization and [Streams](../../../terminology/glossary.md#stream), and shall reject any read or ingest that targets another environment's organization or **Streams**.

## Response Measure

An operational exercise presents a staging [Environment-Scoped Observability Credential](../../../terminology/glossary.md#environment-scoped-observability-credential) to the production organization and its [Streams](../../../terminology/glossary.md#stream).
Every cross-environment read and ingest is rejected, while the same **Environment-Scoped Observability Credential's** reads and ingests in its own environment succeed.
