# QR-019: Isolate Observability Data Across Environments

## Source

A client presents an environment-scoped observability credential.

## Stimulus

The client attempts to read or ingest [Observability Data](../../../terminology/glossary.md#observability-data) in another environment's organization or streams.

## Environment

Staging and production use one shared OpenObserve instance, and each environment has its own organization, streams, and environment-scoped credentials.
Development runs its own instance outside the shared boundary, and test environments are excluded where their documented isolated configuration intentionally differs.

## Artifact

OpenObserve organization and stream access control.

## Response

OpenObserve shall authorize each environment-scoped credential only for its own environment's organization and streams, and shall reject any read or ingest that targets another environment's organization or streams.

## Response Measure

An operational exercise presents a staging credential to the production organization and its streams.
Every cross-environment read and ingest is rejected, while the same credential's reads and ingests in its own environment succeed.
