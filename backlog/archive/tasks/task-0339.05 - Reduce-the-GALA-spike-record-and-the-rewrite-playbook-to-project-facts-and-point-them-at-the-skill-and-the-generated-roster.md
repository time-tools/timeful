## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
## Why

The spike record and the rewrite playbook were written when the skill was thinner and the roster was prose. Both have since been partly superseded and neither was reduced, so the same knowledge now lives in three or four places and the reader cannot tell which one is authoritative.

`server/GALA.md` is roughly 477 lines, of which a large part is per-construct writeups with their diagnostic transcripts. Every one of those constructs is now a row in the skill's construct table, a row in the generated roster, and a probe in the corpus. The transcripts are evidence for a claim that is pinned elsewhere, kept in a document a future agent reads as current guidance.

The playbook is roughly 238 lines whose seven-step skeleton already appears in the `gala-rewrite` skill and, in its decision content, in the project-independent skill's own triage and verification sections. Three copies of one procedure drift, and the playbook is the copy with the most project-specific detail and therefore the fastest to go stale.

## Outcome

Both documents hold only what is specific to this repository, and everything else is reachable in one hop from a document that owns it.

## What stays in the spike record

- The two committable output styles and the committed examples of each. This is a project decision, not a language fact: it is a choice about what this module's generated code is allowed to look like.
- The vendored runtime's provenance, why it is flattened into one module rather than left as a multi-module snapshot, the wiring into the server module, the container-build detail, and the update procedure.
- The evidence that regeneration is deterministic, including the committed hashes.
- What the transpiler actually did to this repository's files, kept explicitly as observations about these files rather than restated as rules.

## What leaves the spike record

Every per-construct rule, diagnostic, and gap identifier. Each removal names where the knowledge now lives, so the reduction is a routing change rather than a deletion. The construct rule belongs to the skill, the census and verdict belong to the generated roster, and the evidence belongs to the probe.

## What stays in the playbook

This repository's wiring: how to invoke the preflight and what each exit status means, what to update when a twin is added and where, the two output styles by reference, and the definition of done for one file as a checklist of this repository's checks.

## What leaves the playbook

The construct knowledge, the decision rules, and the traps table. Those belong to the skill, and a second copy in a project document is what this whole task exists to remove.

## The twin inventory

It exists twice, in the server readme and in the spike record, and the two tables carry overlapping columns. The readme's copy is the one an agent needs, because it carries the regeneration command. The readme's copy is kept as the single copy and is not generated: fifteen stable rows with editorial columns, and the regeneration command is not derivable from a walk of the tree. The spike record's table becomes a pointer.

## Constraints

- The spike record's durable findings for the three spikes it records are not deleted, only relocated, and the record still says which tasks it is the record for.
- Neither document becomes a stub. A reader who opens either must be able to act, and a pointer that does not say what the target owns is not a pointer.
- A project observation that reads like a general rule is either marked as an observation or moved, so a future agent does not generalize from one file's experience. The distinction matters most for the findings that came from this repository's own packages, such as the shape of a specific method set spread across twelve files.
- No document points at a section that no longer exists, and every inbound link still resolves.
- Neither document restates a count that a generated region owns.
- Root Markdown follows the sentence-per-line and table-row rules in `docs/AGENTS.md`.
- The committed twins, the vendored runtime, and the corpus are not modified.

## Evidence required

Produce a table of the removed sections against where each now lives, and confirm no removed item is unreachable.
Confirm by search that no construct-level rule or diagnostic transcript remains in either document.
