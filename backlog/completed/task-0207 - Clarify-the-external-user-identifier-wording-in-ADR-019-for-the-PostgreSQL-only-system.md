## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
ADR-019 "Resolve Accounts By External User Identifier" is accepted and its decision still matches the implementation, but its terminology suggests the account identifier is sourced from an external or legacy store. After the MongoDB retirement, the 24-character hexadecimal identifier is minted by the application inside PostgreSQL (models.NewID().Hex() on new sign-ins), and platform_identities.external_user_id remains the single resolution point for references held by the sign-in session and cross-record columns. Clarify the ADR wording so "external" is explicitly historical naming for a reference identifier external to the accounts row, not a second-store or third-party identifier, and make the no-per-table-identifier-column statement precise.

Scope and constraints:
- Documentation-only change limited to docs/design/architecture/adr/ADR-019.md.
- Keep status accepted, the satisfies FR-121 metadata, the supersession of ADR-012, and the ADR-018 and server/docs/postgres-data-boundaries.md links.
- Do not change the decision itself and do not rewrite superseded or deprecated ADR history (ADR-012 and earlier keep their wording per TASK-0206).
- Implementation evidence for the clarification lives in server/postgres/identities.go, server/postgres/accounts.go, server/accounts/accounts.go, server/models/id.go, server/middleware/auth.go, and server/migrations/20260908160000_visitor_identities.sql.

## Implementation Plan

Research (current system):
- `platform_identities.external_user_id` is `TEXT NOT NULL UNIQUE` (server/migrations/20260908160000_visitor_identities.sql:4).
- `FindOrCreatePlatformIdentity` creates it idempotently (server/postgres/identities.go:14); `GetAccountByExternalUserID` resolves the authoritative account (server/postgres/accounts.go:64).
- A new sign-in mints a 24-hex identifier with `models.NewID().Hex()` (server/accounts/accounts.go:72, server/models/id.go:109); existing accounts keep the migrated hex value.
- The sign-in session stores it (server/routes/auth.go:266) and middleware resolves through it (server/middleware/auth.go:15).
- Cross-record reference columns hold the same string: `postgres_events.owner_external_id`, `account_user_id` in responses/attendees/folders/signup/availability/daily logs, `access_transfers.external_user_id`, `account_deletion_tombstones.external_user_id`.

Steps:
1. Reword ADR-019 Context to define the term: an "external account reference" is a reference by the account's stable 24-hex string identifier instead of the internal UUID; "external" is historical naming and does not mean the identifier is sourced from a second store or a third-party provider.
2. Reword Decision Outcome to state the identifier is application-generated and PostgreSQL-owned, to list the reference sites, and to make the schema claim precise: no generic legacy-identifier mapping table exists, while reference columns store the account identifier directly.
3. Keep the chosen option, the supersession of ADR-012, the ADR-018 link, the FR-121 metadata, and the Consequences.
4. Record that `updated_date` stays 2026-09-11 because the material revision falls on the creation date.
5. Verify with the Markdown checks and `graphify update .`.

Risks: wording must not change the decision or imply compatibility mapping; keep one sentence per physical line per docs/AGENTS.md.
